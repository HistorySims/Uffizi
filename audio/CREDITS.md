# Background music credits

Background music plays quietly during the looking phase. It should be calm and
contemplative first, and a good match for the work second. Tracks were trimmed,
lightly cleaned of surface noise, converted to mono MP3, and leveled to match
the other tracks in this folder.

Recordings released in the US before 1926 are in the public domain. All Library
of Congress recordings below come from its National Jukebox, via Internet Archive.
